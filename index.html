A little note for you

to my once upon a time

happy birthday, you

i hope life gets easier for you this year. semoga semua hal yang lagi lo usahain bisa tercapai, semoga lo selalu dikelilingi hal' baik. i hope you find happiness in the little things, get everything u've been working for, n always have a reason to smile. please take care of yourself, eat well, don't stay up too late, and be kinder to yourself.

gue gatau ini udah hari keberapa, but i still find myself thinking about you. sometimes it makes me happy, knowing that i once got to experience being loved by you. but it also makes me sad knowing that i can't have that anymore.

i know i expected too much sometimes, and maybe i cared more than i should've. but despite everything, i'm still glad our paths crossed.

thank you for being a part of my story, damie. and if there's one thing i wish for, it's that we never cross paths again by accident.

happy birthday yaa. i hope the world is gentle with you.
